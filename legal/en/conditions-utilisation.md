## Article 5: verification of student status

Full access to the resources is reserved for persons who can prove their
enrolment in a higher-education institution.

Where you have an email address issued by your institution, verification is
automatic.

Otherwise, you are asked to submit a supporting document: student card,
enrolment certificate, pass record or registration receipt. This document is
reviewed by an authorised administrator within forty-eight (48) hours. It is
deleted from our servers thirty (30) days after the decision.

Submitting a forged document leads to the rejection of the application, the
closure of the account, and exposes its author to the criminal penalties

In the event of rejection, the reason is communicated to you and you may submit
