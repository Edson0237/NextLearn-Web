# Personal Data Protection Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

## Preamble

This policy is established in accordance with Law No. 2024/017 of
23 December 2024 on the protection of personal data in Cameroon, as well as
Law No. 2010/012 of 21 December 2010 on cybersecurity and cybercrime and
Law No. 2010/021 of 21 December 2010 governing electronic commerce in
Cameroon.

It explains what data NextLearn collects, why, how long it is kept, who it is
shared with, and which rights you can exercise.

Reading it is essential before any registration. Your acceptance is collected
in a free, informed, specific, unambiguous and express manner, through a
checkbox separate from the one for the terms of use.

## Article 2: data we collect

### 2.1 Data you provide to us

- Your email address, your last name and first name.
- Your higher-education institution, your field of study and your level.
- Your display language.
- Your four-digit PIN (optional), stored as an irreversible cryptographic hash. It is never
  stored in clear text and the publisher cannot retrieve it.
- Where you choose to sign in with Google (Google OAuth), your unique Google identifier
  (sub), email address, first and last name provided by Google.
- **Your profile photo, if you choose to upload one.** It is optional: the
  application works identically without it, and you can remove it at any time
  from the Profile screen. A photograph of a face is data that directly
  identifies you, and it is treated as such. It is used for one purpose only:
  to represent you in your own application. It is never shown to other users,
  never used to recognise you automatically, and is not subject to any
  biometric processing: no facial template is computed and no comparison is
  carried out.
- Where you do not have an email address issued by your institution, the
  supporting document you submit to establish your registration: student card,
  enrolment certificate, pass record or registration receipt, and where
  applicable your student number.

### 2.2 Data produced by your use of the service

- The documents you view, your reading progress and the duration of your study
  sessions.
- Your favourites and your downloads for offline use.
- Your results and your answers to self-assessment exercises.
- The ratings you give to documents and exams.
- Your content reports and your messages to support.
- The documents you propose to the catalogue, where applicable, and the rights
  regime you declare for them.

### 2.3 Technical data

- Your IP address, recorded when an administration decision concerns you, so
  that it can be established after the fact who decided what.
- A technical device identifier, if you enable notifications.
- A brief description of the devices from which you open a session (device type
  and system), so that you can recognise them on the "connected devices" screen
  and remotely close a session you do not recognise.

NextLearn uses no advertising device, no third-party tracker, and does not
follow your activity outside the application.

## Article 3: purposes and legal bases of processing

Each processing operation pursues a specific purpose and rests on its own legal
basis:

- Creating and securing your account: performance of the contract between us.
- Verifying your student status: performance of the contract, and the
  legitimate interest in reserving academic content for the audience it is
  intended for.
- Offering you documents matching your course of study: performance of the
  contract.
- Keeping your progress, favourites and results: performance of the contract.
- Displaying your profile photo: your consent, given by the upload itself and
  revocable at any time by deleting it.
- Moderating reported content: legal obligation and legitimate interest.
- Sending you notifications: your consent, revocable at any time.
- Keeping a record of administration decisions: duty of accountability.

No decision producing legal effects with regard to you is taken solely on the
basis of automated processing. We carry out no advertising profiling.

## Article 4: consent and minors

Your consent is collected by an explicit positive action. No box is
pre-ticked. You may withdraw it at any time, without affecting the lawfulness
of earlier processing.

NextLearn is intended for higher-education students. If you are under eighteen
(18) years of age, Cameroonian law requires the prior consent of the person
holding parental authority. In that case you must obtain this consent before
creating an account, and provide it to us on request. Failing that, the account
is closed and the data deleted.

Your data is not sold, rented or transferred for commercial purposes.

It is accessible:

- to the publisher, and to persons the publisher expressly authorises for
  administration tasks, within the limits that task requires;
- to Cameroonian administrative or judicial authorities, on a lawful request
  and within the limits of that request.

We also use the following technical processors, bound by their contractual
terms and required to act only on our instructions:

- Cloudflare, Inc. (Cloudflare R2), for highly available, encrypted object storage
  of educational documents, past papers, exams, contributions and enrolment certificates,
  served exclusively via cryptographically signed temporary URLs;
- Contabo GmbH / Vercel, for hosting the application API server and the PWA;
- MongoDB Atlas, for hosting the database;
- Cloudinary, for hosting profile photos only;
- Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Web Push API, for delivering notifications
  when you have enabled them;
- Google (Google OAuth services and Gmail messaging), for secure authentication,
  login codes (OTP) and service messages.

Your identity or enrolment document is never served from a fixed or guessable address.
Each access goes through a time-limited, cryptographically signed address.

Access to identity documents is restricted to the persons authorised to process
applications, and each access is logged.

## Article 6: transfers outside Cameroon

The providers mentioned in Article 5 operate servers located outside Cameroon.
Your data is therefore subject to an international transfer.

Law No. 2024/017 makes such a transfer subject to the prior authorisation of
the Personal Data Protection Authority (APDP), which verifies that the
destination country ensures an adequate level of protection.

This formality falls on the publisher as data controller, regardless of the
fact that they operate in their own name. The status of the procedure is stated
here as soon as it is completed.

## Article 7: retention periods

- Profile photo: kept for as long as you keep it online. Deleting it from the
  application removes it immediately from our servers and from those of the
  file host; it is not kept in an archive.
- Identity document: automatically deleted thirty (30) days after the decision
  made on your application. A daily task carries out this purge.
- Session tokens: thirty (30) days from the last login.
- Verification codes sent by email: ten (10) minutes.
- Recognition of a device that waives the verification code: the validity
  period of active verification, with no extension through use.
- Account and usage data: kept for as long as the account exists.
- Records of administration decisions: kept after the account is closed, in
  anonymised form, for justification purposes.

## Article 8: security

Exchanges between the application and our servers are encrypted. Your PIN is
hashed. Access to your account requires this PIN, as well as a periodic
verification of your email address. Administration access is subject to
two-factor authentication and named permissions.

We maintain a register of our processing activities, available to the APDP.

As no system is infallible, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Article 9: data breach

In the event of a personal data breach, we notify the APDP without delay. Where
the breach is likely to affect your rights, you are also informed, together
with the measures taken and those we recommend to you.

## Article 10: your rights

You have the following rights over your data:

- Access: obtain confirmation that it is processed and receive a copy.
- Rectification: have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected. Your profile
  information can be edited directly in the application.
- Erasure: obtain the deletion of your data. You can exercise it yourself,
  immediately, from the Profile screen then "Delete my account". Deletion is
  final and removes your favourites, your progress, your results, your reports,
  your profile photo and your supporting document.
- Objection: object to processing on grounds relating to your particular
  situation.
- Restriction: request the suspension of a contested processing while it is
  being verified.
- Portability: receive your data in a structured, commonly used format.
- Objection to profiling: refuse any decision based exclusively on automated
  processing.

These rights are exercised through the address given in Article 1. We reply
within thirty (30) days. We may ask you to prove your identity where there is
reasonable doubt.

## Article 11: complaint

If our reply does not satisfy you, you may refer the matter to the Personal Data
Protection Authority (APDP), the authority competent in Cameroon to deal with
breaches of Law No. 2024/017.

This option does not preclude an appeal before the competent courts.

## Article 12: trackers and local on-device storage

The NextLearn application uses no advertising cookie and no third-party audience
measurement tracker.

Data stored on your device (browser secure storage, IndexedDB, and service worker cache)
is strictly limited to what is necessary for the service to work:
- your active session and display preferences (theme, language);
- documents you have explicitly chosen to keep offline, stored in isolated
  IndexedDB application storage (`nl-stockage-local-db`);
- your local reading progress and recent search history.

All account-related data stored locally on your device (offline documents, reading
progress, recent searches, session tokens) is **completely purged from your device upon
sign-out, account deletion, or when a different account signs in on the same device**,
guaranteeing complete isolation between users of the same terminal.

Consent given to this policy in no way constitutes consent to any advertising trackers,
which would be the subject of a separate agreement.

## Article 13: changes to this policy

Any change leads to the update of the date at the top of this document and to a
notice in the application. Where the change concerns a new purpose, your
consent is collected again before it takes effect.

If the operation of NextLearn were transferred to a company incorporated for
that purpose, you would be informed before the transfer takes effect, and
Article 1 would be updated accordingly.
