# Delete your NextLearn account

Last updated: 8 October 2026

This page describes how to have your NextLearn account and the data attached to
it deleted. It is accessible without being logged in and without installing the
application, in accordance with Google Play requirements.

## The fastest way: from the application

If you still have access to your account, deletion is immediate and requires no
action on our side:

1. Open NextLearn and go to the **Profile** tab.
2. Tap **Delete my account**.
3. Confirm with your four-digit PIN.

Deletion takes effect immediately. There is no cooling-off period and no email
confirmation is needed.

## If you no longer have access to the application

Write to **tientcheumarvin@gmail.com** stating:

- the email address associated with your account;
- your last name and first name as they appear on the account;
- the subject "Account deletion".

Write from the account's email address when possible: it saves us from asking
you for additional proof.

A reasonable doubt about your identity may lead us to ask you for a supporting
document. This check has a single purpose: to prevent a third party from having
your account deleted in your place.

### Timeframe

We acknowledge receipt of your request within **seven (7) days**, and deletion
is carried out within a maximum of **thirty (30) days** from receipt, a period
set by Law No. 2024/017 of 23 December 2024 on the protection of personal data
in Cameroon.

## What is deleted

Deletion is final. It removes:

- your account, your last name, first name and email address;
- your PIN;
- your profile photo;
- your institution, field of study and level;
- your identity or enrolment document, where applicable;
- your favourites and your offline downloads;
- your reading progress and your study sessions;
- your results and your answers to self-assessment exercises;
- your ratings, your reports and your messages to support;
- your session tokens and the technical identifier of your devices;
- your notification token.

## What is kept, and why

Only two categories remain after deletion:

- **Records of administration decisions concerning you**, kept in **anonymised**
  form - they no longer allow you to be identified. They meet the obligation to
  be able to establish after the fact who decided what.
- **Documents you proposed to the catalogue**, where applicable, if they were
  admitted. They stay online detached from your account: they are no longer
  linked to you, and nothing indicates that you are their author. If you want
  them removed and not merely detached, say so in your request.

No backup copy of your account is kept beyond deletion.

## Other rights over your data

Deletion is not your only right. You can also request access to your data,
their rectification, their portability, or object to a processing operation.
These rights, how to exercise them and the complaint routes are set out in
Article 10 of our [data protection policy](/confidentialite/).
